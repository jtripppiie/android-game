Moto Thrash: Retro Racing Privacy Policy

TripperDeeLabs · Effective October 8, 2026

Game data

Moto Thrash: Retro Racing is an offline game developed by TripperDeeLabs. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, in-app purchases or online multiplayer.

Local progress and custom tracks

Race progress, unlocked tracks, records, medals, sound preferences, custom courses and Stunt Show settings and personal bests are saved in the app's private storage on your device. The game does not send these saves to TripperDeeLabs or other parties. There is no cloud save or online track sharing.

Storage and deletion

Local saves remain until you clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. Doing so permanently removes the local data. Android app backup is disabled in the game's configuration. We do not hold server copies of your progress or custom courses and cannot restore deleted saves.

Support email

If you contact feedback@tripperdeelabs.com, your email provider sends us your address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use this correspondence to respond to your request and retain it as needed to resolve the request and related follow-up, or meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the same address. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Google Play

Google Play handles purchases and downloads under Google's own privacy policy. TripperDeeLabs does not receive your payment-card information through the game.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian can contact us about information supplied in a support request.

This website

This policy describes the game. Visiting tripperdeelabs.com is covered separately by our website privacy policy, including website hosting and optional analytics. This app-policy page does not load analytics scripts.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests to delete support correspondence, contact TripperDeeLabs at feedback@tripperdeelabs.com. Changes to the game's data handling will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
