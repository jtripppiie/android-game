Kick up dirt in Moto Thrash: Retro Racing, an offline arcade motocross game built around big jumps, bike balance and boost timing.

RACE 24 TRACKS
Work through four cups, race computer opponents and chase your best times. Manage engine heat, change lanes and lean into clean landings.

BALANCE ON THE EDGE
Pull rear-wheel wheelies and front-wheel balances with the directional pad. Push too far and you will lose stability. Keep your bike level in the air to stick the landing.

BUILD YOUR OWN COURSES
Create tracks in six local save slots. Place ramps, bumps, oil, mud, boost pads and jump hazards, then test your course yourself or watch the computer ride it. Water, rivers, crocodiles, lava, cars and buses bring more challenges to your designs.

TAKE ON STUNT SHOW
Choose up to 20 buses, set your launch ramp and run-up speed, and attempt a clean landing. Earn points and beat your personal best.

RETRO STYLE, TOUCH CONTROLS
Pixel riders, trackside trees and an unusual stadium crowd bring the course to life. Play with an on-screen directional pad and boost button, with animated engine feedback and synthesized arcade sound effects.

No ads, no in-app purchases and no account required. Progress and custom courses stay on your device. Single-player only; custom tracks are saved locally, with no online sharing or cloud saves.
